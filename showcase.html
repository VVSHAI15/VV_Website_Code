The Composer

AviShai Dayanim is a composer, orchestrator, producer, and storyteller based in Los Angeles. A Dual Degree graduate of the Peabody Institute and Johns Hopkins University, he holds a Bachelor of Music in Music for New Media and a Bachelor of Science in Computer Science, a pairing that shapes everything from the way he scores to the tools he builds.

From an early age, AviShai has been captivated by music's power to tell stories and evoke deep emotion. He has composed scores for short films, theater productions, and video games, earning multiple festival awards.

That same curiosity drives his technical work. In 2023, AviShai began crafting virtual instruments through an internship at Impact Soundworks, designing unique sample libraries that bring new dimensions to his compositions. Today he works as a technical score assistant to composer Henry Jackman (Captain America, X-Men, Kingsman). Along the way he has also collaborated with composers including Michael Levine (Megamind, The Simpsons Movie) and Mac Quayle (Mr. Robot, American Horror Story).

— AviShai
